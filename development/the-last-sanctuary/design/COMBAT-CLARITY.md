## Problem

Attack effects are obscure, like the Quantum Draft feedback problem. Small bottom text carries too much explanatory duty. The large cards currently provide only magic, rather than the understandable field decisions and consequences expected from Lineage.

## Approved order

1. Combat feedback on the board first. Before confirmation, highlight attacker and target, connect them with a clear attack line, label the attack and damage, preview target health (e.g. 3 to 1), and show action cost beside Confirm. During/after attacks, show a short strike or connecting bolt, hit flash, floating damage, persistent small health bars, unmistakable defeat animation/removal. Announce Raiders' turn and show enemy actions individually. Bottom text becomes secondary battle history.
2. Shrink spells into a compact selected-unit ability tray. Preserve the approved magic effects and direct board interaction; large spell-only cards should stop obstructing the board.
3. Add a small set of contextual Lineage-style tactical decision cards with real mechanical tradeoffs. Example: Hold the Crossing lets Aldric brace at the bridge, forgoing movement for protection while Seren repositions. Example: Press the Advantage commits the remaining action to a stronger attack at the cost of retreat. These examples require supporting mechanics; cards must change decisions, not merely restate available moves. Exact balance remains to be designed.

Use consistent shapes and labels alongside color for movement, attack targets, threatened ground, protection and blocked routes. Selecting a piece reveals useful information; selecting an order focuses the board on that decision.

This is the approved hybrid approach: compact magic abilities plus larger contextual tactical choices. Current baseline is the local 25-hex skirmish revision14, preferred Rampart option2, drag/tap confirmation, approved fixed-view art and connected water.
