# Battle I: The Broken Crossing

The Last Sanctuary — complete first mission, October 25, 2026. Local playtest; not released.

## Premise

Ash raiders hold the northern wayshrine. The main Ash host will reach the crossing after twelve rounds. Seren and Aldric must recover its relic and return it to the southern sanctuary. This is new scenario lore developed for the first battle, not a previously established campaign history.

## Complete 35-hex battlefield

Columns 1–7 run west to east; rows A–E run north to south. Rows are staggered hexes.

| Row | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Woodland | Woodland | Meadow | Captain | Relic shrine | Ruined watch | Bowman |
| B | Woodland | Meadow | Scout | Meadow | Meadow | Reaver | Meadow |
| C | River | Western ford | River | Stone bridge | River | River | River |
| D | Meadow | Meadow | Meadow | Meadow | Meadow | Meadow | Woodland |
| E | Keeper cottage | Sanctuary | Seren | Aldric | Meadow | Meadow | Woodland |

The cottage is scenery; woodland, ruins, shrine and river are impassable. The shrine is accessed from an adjacent hex. The western ford and central bridge provide two physical return routes.

## Turn rules

Three shared actions and three refreshed mana each player round. Each move, attack or spell costs one action. Any hero can use multiple shared actions. Press the Advantage costs two actions and adds one damage. End Turn passes any unused actions. Then each surviving enemy moves OR attacks once. Ranged attacks use hex distance and arc over terrain; no random hit chance.

Seren: 6 HP, 1 damage, range 2. Aldric: 9 HP, 2 damage, range 1. Scout and Reaver: 2 HP, 1 damage, range 1. Bowman: 3 HP, 1 damage, range 2. Captain: 4 HP, 2 damage, range 1.

Fold the Path: Seren moves 2–4 hexes for 2 mana. Living Rampart: blocks an empty land hex within range 3 for 1 mana; enemies can break a wall if trapped. Echo Ward: 1 mana; blocks the next hit on Aldric. Hold the Crossing: Aldric on C4 takes one less damage per hit and cannot move until the next player turn.

## Relic and outcome

A hero adjacent to A5 automatically claims the relic. The carrier cannot portal. A fallen carrier drops the relic on their hex; the surviving hero can recover it by entering that hex. Return the carrier to E2 to win, even if enemies survive. Defeating all enemies does not complete the mission. Lose both heroes or end round twelve without returning the relic to lose. Restart restores the whole encounter. No campaign progression or save/resume is included in this first mission.

## Play

Serve the project directory and open battle.html. The launch page selects the complete illustrated board and battle mode automatically. A destination/target menu provides an alternative to small board targets, especially on phones. Orders still require confirmation.
