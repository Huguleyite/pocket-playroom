## Asset provenance and art direction

The four new assets were generated individually using imagegen with Seren’s PNG as the style reference. Preserve natural proportions, detailed faces, deep teal and ivory cloth, weathered antique gold and steel, engraved bronze bases with slate/moss footings, a front three-quarter camera from above, transparent backgrounds and warm upper-left studio lighting. Distinct weapons, posture and profiles distinguish the roles at small sizes. Retain the existing diverse character lineup.

These are fixed-view illustrated miniatures. Their baked appearance does not relight or rotate as a full sculpt. The new assets are isolated PNGs; no 3D model is claimed. Shared palette and bases are the target, rather than exact pixel-identical proportions. Additional directions, alternate factions and new animation poses remain later work.
