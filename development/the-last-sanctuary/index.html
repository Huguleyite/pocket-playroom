WELCOME TO THE TABLE

Make a little magic.

This is a small, interactive visual study of The Last Sanctuary.

  1. Card first: choose Fold the Path, then a glowing destination, then Open the portal.
  2. Drag: drag a miniature or its nameplate onto open land, then confirm the preview. Works with touch or mouse. An invalid drop makes no move.
  3. Board first: select Seren, then choose a destination. A nearby hex selects a free move; a distant hex selects Fold the Path.
  4. Try Living Rampart on an empty tile, or Echo Ward on Aldric.

Previews are free. Confirming a spell spends mana. Reset study restores everything.

Keyboard: Tab through cards, miniatures and land hexes; Enter selects. Escape cancels an order. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
