Vault Breakers

Plan the heist. Crack the relics. Escape together.

Cooperative play for 1–4 players. Secure every relic and get all four operatives into the airlock before the alert reaches 100%.

Try the playtestHow to play

Set up your crew

Shared screen + phones

Open the game on the shared screen. Players scan its QR code or use the displayed room code to join.

Solo or pass-and-play

Select “Solo / Local Pass-and-Play” in the lobby. Use the operative tabs to plan for Cipher, Forge, Lynx and Ghost on the same device.

Choose a mission

Rookie has one relic and two guards. Pro adds a second relic and cameras. Mastermind adds a third relic, another guard and extra lasers.

How to play

  1. Plan

    Each operative chooses a move and optionally equips one gadget, then locks the plan.

  2. Resolve

    Gadgets fire, the crew moves, and guards and cameras patrol.

  3. Watch the alert

    Being seen adds 25% alert. Standing in a laser beam also adds 25%. At 100%, the vault locks down.

  4. Escape together

    Crack each relic’s case, then step onto it to collect it. Once every relic is secured, get all four operatives into the extraction airlock.

Help test the heist

This is a playtest. Try the setup that suits your crew and note anything confusing, any joining issues, and which mission you played.
