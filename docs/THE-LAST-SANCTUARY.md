# The Last Sanctuary — five-battle campaign

Released October 25, 2026 as the fifth Full Desktop game in Pocket Playroom.

The campaign uses an expanded 63-hex (9 × 7) battlefield, up from 35. The five engagements share the river-crossing terrain, with different objectives and tougher enemy companies.

1. The Broken Crossing: retrieve the shrine relic and return it to sanctuary G3, within 14 rounds.
2. The Village Exodus: hold Aldric on bridge D5 through three enemy turns, within 12 rounds.
3. The Captain’s Standard: defeat the Ash Captain within 12 rounds.
4. Two Keepers: deliver the relic with BOTH heroes alive, within 14 rounds.
5. The Last Sanctuary: defeat all enemies AND deliver the relic, within 18 rounds.

Heroes recover fully for each new battle, gain two maximum health per battle, and gain one attack from battle three. Enemies gain maximum health across the campaign. Three shared actions and three mana refresh each round. Battle 2 secured turns accumulate rather than requiring an uninterrupted hold. The carrier cannot portal.

Completed battles unlock the next mission. The battle menu permits replay of unlocked missions; Restart mission starts that mission fresh. Progress and the current battle save in local storage, subject to browser storage availability. A completed final battle remains available for replay. No multiplayer is included.
