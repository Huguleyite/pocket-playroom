# Content and playtest notices

October 25, 2026. User authorized adding a development/testing notice to The Last Sanctuary and entertainment/non-affiliation language to Pocket Playroom About.

Notices clarify purpose, creative intent, independence, third-party rights and unfinished playtest behavior. They do not assert an infringement audit, fair-use determination, trademark clearance, immunity from claims, or attorney approval. Existing experience-specific disclosures remain in place.

Research: https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html ; https://www.uspto.gov/page/about-trademark-infringement ; https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html . Copyright can protect expressive game assets even though ideas and playing methods are excluded. Confusion over source or sponsorship remains a trademark concern. Fair use is fact-specific. Actual risk assessment requires reviewing each title, asset, and use, preferably with qualified IP counsel.
