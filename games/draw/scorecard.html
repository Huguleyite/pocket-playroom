Six boxes. Real choices.

  1. Play a hand card into an empty space. Then draw one visible card or take an unknown card.
  2. Select table cards and choose an eligible unused category. Only those cards clear. Each category scores once.
  3. Straights and flushes need five cards. A–2–3–4–5 and 10–J–Q–K–A are valid straights; other wraparound is not.
  4. When stuck, select up to five cards, turn on Sacrifice, and choose an unused category. Confirm to close it for zero points and discard those cards. You may sacrifice with no cards selected to close a box.
  5. Finish all six categories, scored or sacrificed. Aim for all six scored and 360 points. Undo is unlimited.

This prototype uses one standard 52-card deck with no recycling. The guided deal begins with five cards already on the table. Shuffled rounds start empty.

Classic remains unchanged, including its three-card combinations and ordered six-card run bonus. This Scorecard prototype focuses on the six categories; four of a kind, straight flush bonuses and redraws are reserved for a later balance pass.
