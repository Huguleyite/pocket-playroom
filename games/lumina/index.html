How to play LUMINA

Play and reshape tiles
Strike
Tap or click a tile to light it and play a note.
Harp sweep
In Drift, sweep immediately in any direction, including sideways. Faster sweeps make brighter strikes. In Pulse or Harmony, start a sideways sweep in a gutter.
Slide
In Drift, press a tile briefly before dragging sideways. Bars can overlap slightly when crowded and separate when you let go. In Pulse or Harmony, drag sideways right away. Quick collisions can chime.
Morph
Double-tap a tile in Drift or Harmony, scroll over it with a mouse wheel, or turn MORPH on and tap in any mode. Each action cycles its width. Narrow tiles sound an octave higher and wide ones an octave lower.
Drift, Pulse, and Harmony
Drift
Play freely. Try a quick diagonal sweep, then drag one glowing tile sideways.
Pulse
Tap a tile to add it to the loop. Tap it again to take it out. Sweep across tiles to add several. The beam plays the loop about once every 3.2 seconds. RESET clears the whole pattern.
Harmony
Tiles show their matching colors even when idle. Slide matching colors until their centers line up across at least three neighboring tracks. A white-blue surge and a major-ninth chord mark the match.
Scales, voices, and volume
Scale
Tap SCALE to cycle F Lydian, C Pentatonic, E-flat Major / C Minor, D Dorian, Hirajoshi, and Whole Tone. Each change plays a preview.
Voice
Tap VOICE to preview and cycle Glass Chime, Felt Piano, Analog Pad, Marimba Wood, and FM Celesta.
Sound and volume
SOUND mutes and unmutes live playback and WAV previews. The slider sets their volume. Recording captures the synth at its original level.
Reset
Clear tile charges and restore the starting widths and positions. Your mode, scale, voice, and sound settings stay selected.
Record and save a WAV
Start and stop
Tap REC 10s, then play the board. The countdown stops at ten seconds, or you can tap REC again to finish early.
Preview and save
Use PLAY or STOP in the recording card. The browser may download the stereo WAV automatically. Use SAVE .WAV to save it manually.
