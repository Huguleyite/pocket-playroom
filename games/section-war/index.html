    How to play

    Tap an attack, then tap your rival. Mute also needs a target. Block and utility cards resolve when tapped. Block expires at the start of its owner's turn. Long Reach adds 2 to your first single-target attack each turn. Squeak makes your next attack miss and expires at the end of the turn. Tempo Brake reduces the next attack that would hit by 4. Duct Tape clears Squeak and Out of Tune. Fortified adds 3 to the next Block gain. Out of Tune deals 2 damage per stack at the start of a turn, then loses one stack. Knock your rival to 0 HP to win.
