    Field guide

    Win 7 tricks to secure the anomaly. Each trick is worth one point. You lead the first trick; each winner leads the next. When responding, follow the led sector if you can. Bright cards are legal moves.

    Resolution & expedition rules

    Both cards commit before abilities resolve. Bio 9 silences the opposing card first. Cyber shifts resolve in play order before rank mutations and victory checks. Trump beats the led suit; the led suit beats other sectors. Equal ranks favor the leader. Void inversions can beat a high non-trump even if that card follows the led suit. A winning Void 8 cancels nothing; a losing Void 8 cancels only the winner’s post-trick abilities.

    Cyber 4 changes trump for this trick only. Other Cyber shifts persist. Cyber 7 locks all later trump changes through the expedition. Mutations are temporary and may exceed rank 9. Bio 1 copies the opponent’s printed rank. Bio 6 swaps current effective ranks. Void 9 checks effective ranks, so its even/even condition requires mutation.

    Flip the first reserve card to set trump; it leaves the reserve. Empty reserve effects do nothing. If either hand empties, remaining hands are discarded and a fresh 36-card expedition begins; scores carry over and the last trick winner leads. Locks reset.

    Discard choices & open information

    Solar 5 lets the affected player choose a discard. Nexus discards its lowest-value card. Solar 7 discards randomly; Solar 8 discards the lowest printed rank (sector order breaks ties). A discard always comes from the remaining hand, never a committed card. Void 4 lets you choose from the opponent’s discard pile. Nexus chooses its highest-value recovery. Captured trick cards are separate from discards.

    Solar 4 reveals the highest printed-rank card, to the player who used it. Solar 6 privately reveals the next anomaly to its winner. Telemetry never reveals Nexus’s private discoveries. All public ability results appear in the stream.
