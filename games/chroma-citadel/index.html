Controls & instructions

Select a block, then tap or click the grid or a cube face to place it. On mobile, use Change block to open the tray.

Stack 3 matching blocks vertically to activate Pyre, Bastion, Siphon, or Pulse Mine abilities. Obsidian provides armor.

Drag to rotate the build view. On desktop, scroll to zoom and use keys 1–6 to select a tool. Demolish refunds half the block cost.

Start Wave when ready. During battle, drag to look. Press and hold the front window to see enemies through your blocks. Use Exterior view to watch your defenses.

Protect both entrances. A window or door breach ends the game.
