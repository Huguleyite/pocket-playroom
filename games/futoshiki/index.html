How to play

Use 1–5 exactly once in every row and column. The open side of each sign faces the larger number. A vertical ∧ means the top number is smaller; ∨ means it is larger.

Tap a filled cell, then Erase or Clear selected cell to leave it blank. Starting clues stay fixed. Reset board clears all your entries and resets today’s timer and stats after confirmation. Keyboard: arrow keys to move, 1–5 to enter, and Backspace or Delete to erase. Conflicting entries stay editable and briefly glow red.

The daily puzzle changes at midnight in your local time. The timer counts time while this page is visible.
