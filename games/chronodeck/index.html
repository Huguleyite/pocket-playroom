FIRST MISSION // FIELD GUIDE

Survive the breach.

Cross three acts and 21 floors. Defeat the Chrono Sentinel, Archive Warden and Paradox Sovereign. Between acts, choose a relic and repair your hull. You lose when your HP reaches zero.

01 / Choose your route

Click a glowing map node. Clear the encounter to unlock connected paths. Combat gives credits and a choice of cards; Elites are tougher. Shops sell cards and relics and offer card removal. Rest sites heal; Events offer choices.

02 / Spend your energy

Each turn, draw 5 cards and refill to 3 energy. A card’s corner number is its cost. Click an Attack, then an enemy, or drag it onto the target. For self or all-enemy cards, select the card and click Play selected card, or drag it into the arena.

03 / Prepare for the enemy

Later fights can include several enemies, each with its own intent. “All enemies” cards affect the whole group. Block absorbs attack damage in the upcoming enemy phase, then resets next turn. HP carries between fights. Click End turn when ready: your remaining hand is discarded, and enemy actions due Now resolve.

04 / Bend the timeline

The track shows Now plus four future turns. Delay moves an enemy’s next action two turns later. Loop replays the last non-Loop card from your previous turn; it cannot work on turn one. Click a timeline slot for details.

Start here: Block the incoming attack, then spend remaining energy on Pulse Strikes. Your discard pile reshuffles when needed. Exhausted cards stay out until combat ends. Relics act automatically; some change your energy or draw.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Refresh to resume. New run replaces your save after confirmation.
