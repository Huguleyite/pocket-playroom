Send a signal.

Connect the blue source to the red receiver across 30 puzzles.

  1. Trace a route. The pulse moves between lit blocks in neighboring columns. Tall blocks span two rows and let the pulse switch rows.
  2. Change the board. Tap a relay to switch it on or off. Use the row arrows or swipe to slide all blocks marked ↔ in that row together, one column at a time.
  3. Spend moves on the route. Each toggle or slide costs one move. Spare relays and dead ends can stay on or off. Plan backward from the receiver if you see several branches.
  4. Test your route. Press Send for free. Watch which blocks the pulse reaches. Use Undo to take back a move or Reset to start the puzzle over.
