OPERATOR’S GUIDE

Follow the signal.

  • ▲ Source: fixed on the edge, sending light inward.
  • ◎ Target: receive only the matching color. A wrong-color arrival turns the ring red and blocks completion.
  • │ Straight / └ corner: connect the visible ports to carry or bend light.
  • ┬ Splitter: sends light through both other connected ports.
  • ◇ Filter: required at every target; passes only its own color.
  • All three colors share junctions. Rotating one can disconnect several signals at once.

Tap any of the 16 interior prisms for a clockwise quarter turn. Every tap counts as one move. Edge cells stay fixed. You can also use Tab and Enter or Space.
