A LITTLE ROOM BETWEEN STARS

How to play

  1. Place one star in every row.
  2. Place one star in every column.
  3. Place one star in every colored region.
  4. Stars cannot touch, even diagonally.

One starting star is locked. Dots are optional notes: tap empty → dot → star → empty. Turn off “Use dots” in Settings to tap empty → star → empty. Hold any star, including the starting star, to dot the empty cells in its row and column. Existing stars and notes stay as they are. Undo removes the whole batch. On a keyboard, focus a star and press Shift+Enter.

Use Undo anytime. Check explains conflicts without giving answers. Hint places a correct star and is recorded in your result. A fresh puzzle arrives at midnight Eastern.
