    FIRST REIGN // ROYAL FIELD GUIDE

    Five chapters. One unforgettable reign.

    Complete 25 decrees to win your reign. Each decree advances one year. Any meter reaching 0 or 100 ends your dynasty.

    01 / Rule five years at a time

    Each chapter lasts five decisions and ends in a special crisis. Clear years 5, 10, 15, and 20 to choose rewards: a royal favor, a title and balance ward, a landmark, then heir training. Your next reward is always visible on the chapter track.

    02 / Read before you decree

    Choose among four proposals. Select a card, inspect the full council forecast, then Commit decree. Hover or focus previews meter directions. A–D selects; Enter commits; Escape clears selection. On touchscreens, tap a card and then Commit.

    03 / Use the tools you earn

    Favors cost no year: postpone the next obligation two years, redraw a regular petition, or move a meter up to 12 points toward 50. Chapter crises cannot be redrawn. Balance wards pull their meter toward 50 each decree. Landmarks offer ongoing benefits. Favor storage caps at three of each kind.

    04 / Win, then choose your legacy

    Year 25 ends in a victory celebration and a reign rating based on your average meter balance. Finish proudly, or continue through an heir into another 25-year challenge. Rewards and obligations carry forward; new chapters offer more rewards. An early ruler death requires succession but keeps chapter progress.

    Your first goal: Survive five decisions and earn your first royal favor. Deferred obligations resolve in the year shown. Inherited traits add up to +3 per meter yearly; era pressure rises after each 25 dynasty decisions, up to ±3. Reach 75 years only if you want the optional long-dynasty achievement.

    Progress saves in this browser after each decree, reward, and favor. The guide opens before your first game. New dynasty replaces your current run after you confirm its name.
