Swipe horizontally to shift a row, or vertically to shift a column. Letters wrap to the opposite edge. Each swipe moves one space.

Make four-letter words from left to right or top to bottom. Matching lines clear together; letters fall and new ones arrive.

Each word earns 100 points. Consecutive cascade waves multiply that by 2, 3, and so on. Start with 60 seconds and find as many words as you can. Every word adds 2 seconds, including each word in a cascade. Repeated words count. The clock keeps running during hints, instructions, and time away from the game. The round ends at zero.
