# Offline word acceptance

Accepts 3,903 ENABLE words of four letters, plus all original game words. Case insensitive; letters only. Original curated words remain the generation/hint pool.

Source: https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt
ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon) is a public-domain word list. Includes inflections and less common words; no runtime network requests. Downloaded October 21, 2026.
